Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10/31/2026. Community United Network ("CUN," "we," "us") operates this website and related apps. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who can use CUN

CUN is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will delete it.

2. Information you give us

  • Account: email address, display name, date of birth, and a password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way scrambled value (hash), never in readable form.
  • Profile: role (need help, can help, or both), city, state, ZIP code, skills, tools you own, availability, bio, and your chosen message language.
  • Community directory: if you choose to be listed, your display name, role, city, state, skills, tools, bio, and verified status are shown to other members. Your email, date of birth, ZIP code, and street address are never shown in the directory.
  • Tasks: task descriptions, locations, schedules, and the exact address you enter. The exact address is shown only to the requester and helpers assigned to that task.
  • Messages: the text of messages you send to other members, and any reports you file about messages.
  • Identity verification: Stripe checks a government ID and a selfie for you. Stripe collects those images directly; CUN does not receive or store the photos. CUN receives only the result (verified, or needs review) and the date of the check. [Confirm how long Stripe keeps the images and state that in this policy.]
  • Safety reports: descriptions you write about unsafe conditions.

3. Payment and identity providers

Payments, payouts, and identity checks are handled by Stripe, Inc. We do not store your full card number or bank account number. Stripe collects that information directly and processes it under its own privacy policy. We receive limited details from Stripe, such as payment status, amounts, and a payout account identifier.

4. Information collected automatically

We use a session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary for the service. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics on this site. Our hosting provider (Render) keeps standard server logs, which may include IP addresses and request times.

5. How we use information

  • To create and run your account, match people with tasks, and show the community directory you chose to join.
  • To process payments, hold funds, pay helpers, and refund requesters.
  • To keep the community safe: checking reports, pausing tasks when safety is at risk, and removing accounts that break our rules.
  • To send essential messages about your account, tasks, and payments.

6. Messages and translation

Messages are stored so you and the other member can read them. Phone numbers and email addresses are blocked in messages. If translation is turned on, the text of a message is sent to Google (Google Cloud Translation) so it can be shown in the reader's language. Google processes that text under its own terms. 7. Who we share information with

  • Other members: only the information you choose to show in the directory, the task details you post, and messages you send.
  • Stripe: payment and payout processing.
  • Google: message translation, if turned on.
  • Render: website and database hosting.
  • Authorities: when required by law, or to protect someone's safety.

We do not sell your personal information.
